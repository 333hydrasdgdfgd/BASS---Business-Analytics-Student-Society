BASS Organization Merit System

Official Guidelines • Effective Academic Year 2026–2027

Purpose

This system promotes accountability, rewards engagement, and provides tangible benefits for active membership. By classifying events and tracking points, we ensure fairness for all members.

1. Event Classification

Before every event (General Meeting, Social, Fundraiser, etc.), the organization will post an Event Classification notice.

Class Label Definition Demerit Risk
Class 1 Required Attendance is mandatory (e.g., officers, committee heads, members on probation). Yes
Class 2 Encouraged Strongly recommended for all active members. Yes
Class 3 Optional Open to anyone with no attendance penalty. No

Why Classes?

This structure promotes clarity, fairness, and inclusivity by ensuring everyone understands expectations before an event takes place.

2. Point & Demerit System

Points and demerits are not fixed for all events. Instead, they vary depending on the event’s importance. For example:

  • A major General Meeting may award +5 points for attendance and –5 demerits for an unexcused absence.
  • A small social event might award +2 points for attendance and –2 demerits for absence (if classified as Class 1 or 2).
  • Participation bonuses also scale with event importance (e.g., +1 to +3 points).

The exact point values for each event will be announced together with the Event Classification notice (posted at least 48 hours before the event).

Class 3 events never result in demerits for absence, regardless of importance.

3. Valid Excuses

If you cannot attend a Class 1 or Class 2 event, you may submit proof to avoid demerits.

Accepted Reasons

  • Illness (doctor's note or timestamped clinic/OTC receipt)
  • Family emergency
  • University-sanctioned activity
  • Religious observance
  • Work conflict (maximum of 2 per semester)

Submission Process

  1. Email or message the VP of Internal Affairs within 48 hours after the event.
  2. Attach supporting proof.
  3. Use subject format: [EXCUSE] Your Name – Event Name

Approved excuses result in 0 points (no attendance point and no demerit).

4. Semester Rankings & Rewards

At the end of each semester, the organization will publish official rankings based on net points earned (total points minus demerits). Top-ranking members will receive the following rewards:

  • Immunity on second semester org fee — full waiver of the organizational fee for the following semester.
  • Exemption from BASS‑related events — may skip specified BASS activities without penalty.

Note: Exemptions apply only to BASS events/activities and are separate from Council activities (e.g., university‑wide student council events). Rewards expire at the end of the following semester.

5. Important Rules

  • Event classifications (Class 1/2/3) and point values are final once posted 48 hours before an event.
  • Demerits apply only to Class 1 and Class 2 events.
  • Members must sign in/out to receive attendance credit.
  • Members with -10 or lower net points enter Social Probation.
  • Social Probation members may not attend recreational events until returning to positive standing.

Questions?

Contact the BASS Vice President for Internal Affairs for concerns, appeals, or clarification regarding the Merit System.
